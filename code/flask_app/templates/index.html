The Story Game - online

Welcome to the Story Game online! This is a basic web app developed by Bryan Loertscher specifically for use by family and personal friends.
It models the classic Loertscher family get-together tradition of The Story Game, a game in which the players sit in a circle around a table, each writing a part of a story and passing it on to the next person, who continues the story where they left off.
Now that the Loertscher family is spread far and wide, I thought it would be fun to create a version of the game that allows us to span state lines and national boarders in our quest to find out what happened next.
Enjoy!
